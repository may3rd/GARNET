Tablet
768 – 1279 px · drawn at 1024 × 768

Two panes: canvas plus one inspector; the queue is a drawer
Canvas tools grow to 40 px for a finger or stylus
Portrait stacks the panes; landscape keeps them side by side
Desktop
≥ 1280 px · drawn at 1440 × 1024
Icon rail plus three panes: queue, canvas, inspector
Full tables with every column
36 px controls, hover states, keyboard shortcuts
The only width where all four gates are fully editable
The line is drawing versus judging. A phone can accept, reject, defer or pick a value — those are decisions. It cannot place a port on a 30 000 px raster or bridge a 50 px gap in a trace, so those gates send you to a tablet instead of shipping a version that fails in the field.
